About the Department

The Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad is one of the most prestigious CS departments in Pakistan. Established in 1998, the department offers cutting-edge programs at undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral levels.

Our mission is to produce highly skilled, ethically grounded, and research-driven computer scientists who can lead innovation in the technology sector both locally and globally.

Degree Programs Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)
  • Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE)
  • Bachelor of Science in Data Science (BSDS)
  • Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS)
  • Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Science (PhD CS)
