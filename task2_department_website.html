Department of Computer Science & Software Engineering

Faculty of Information Sciences and Technology — National Center of Academic Excellence

Department Overview & Vision

The Department of Computer Science & Software Engineering is dedicated to providing state-of-the-art education and research opportunities in computing sciences. Established with the vision of producing high-caliber engineers, researchers, and innovators, the department fosters a collaborative learning environment equipped with modern computing laboratories, high-performance computing clusters, and specialized artificial intelligence research hubs.

Offered Degree Programs

The department currently offers accredited undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs:

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) — 4 Years
  • Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE) — 4 Years
  • Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (BSAI) — 4 Years
  • Bachelor of Science in Cyber Security (BS-CYS) — 4 Years
  • Master of Science in Computer Science (MSCS) — 2 Years
  • Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Science (PhD CS) — 3 to 5 Years

Semester Core Courses (Advanced Undergraduate Level)

Below is the approved sequence of advanced semester courses starting from Semester 3 (Roman Numeral Format):

  1. Data Structures and Algorithms
  2. Object-Oriented Programming (Java & C++)
  3. Database Management Systems & SQL Architecture
  4. Computer Organization and Assembly Language
  5. Theory of Automata & Formal Languages
  6. Web Technologies & Full-Stack Frameworks
  7. Operating Systems & Concurrency Control
  8. Artificial Intelligence & Neural Networks
