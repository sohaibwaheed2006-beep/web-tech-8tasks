About Our Culinary House

Founded in 2012, Spice Route Gourmet Bistro & Grill brings together the finest culinary traditions from South Asia, the Mediterranean, and the Orient. Every single dish is prepared daily from farm-fresh local produce, ethically sourced meats, and artisan ground spice blends to deliver an unforgettable dining experience.

Featured Chef's Menu Specialties

Explore our most celebrated kitchen offerings crafted by master chefs:

  • Royal Dum Chicken Biryani — Aged aromatic basmati rice layered with tender saffron chicken and caramelized onions.
  • Traditional Handi Mutton Karahi — Succulent goat cuts slow-cooked in a wok with fresh tomatoes, ginger, and green chilies.
  • Smoked Seekh Kebab Platter — Minced lean beef skewers seasoned with coriander, cumin, and grilled over charcoal.
  • Dal Makhani Artisan Pot — Black lentils and kidney beans simmered overnight with organic churned butter and cream.
  • Charred Paneer Tikka Skewers — Fresh cottage cheese cubes marinated in spiced yogurt and roasted in a clay tandoor.
  • Chilled Alfonso Mango Lassi — Sweet mango pulp blended with rich artisanal whole-milk yogurt.

Our Authentic 5-Step Meal Preparation Process

We adhere to strict culinary standards to guarantee unparalleled taste and hygiene:

  1. Source Fresh Ingredients: Select premium organic vegetables and grass-fed meats delivered fresh each morning.
  2. Artisan Spice Grinding: Roast whole dry spices and blend them in-house for signature fragrance and depth.
  3. Deep Marinade: Immerse meats and vegetarian proteins in spiced herb marinades for a minimum of 6 hours.
  4. Traditional Flame & Tandoor Cooking: Slow-braise curries in heavy copper vessels and sear kebabs in a 450°C clay tandoor.
  5. Garnish & Quality Plating: Finish each dish with freshly chopped cilantro, julienned ginger, and house-made mint chutney.
