Our Menu

  • Grilled Seekh Kebab — Minced beef skewers with herbs
  • Paneer Tikka — Marinated cottage cheese grilled in tandoor
  • Mango Lassi — Chilled yogurt mango blend

Our Preparation Process

  1. Source fresh, locally procured ingredients every morning.
  2. Marinate proteins with handcrafted spice blends for minimum 4 hours.
  3. Slow-cook curries and gravies over traditional flame for depth of flavour.
  4. Finish dishes in a tandoor clay oven at 450°C for authentic char.
  5. Plate with garnishes of fresh coriander, lemon, and house chutney.
