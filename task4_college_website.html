About Horizon Crest Institute of Technology

Established in 1994, Horizon Crest Institute of Technology has evolved into one of the nation's premier institutions for undergraduate and postgraduate education in engineering, computing, and applied sciences. Our campus combines cutting-edge academic research facilities with an environment dedicated to intellectual curiosity and ethical leadership.

We do not limit education to theoretical classroom lectures; instead, we provide direct immersion in state-of-the-art laboratory environments, industry apprenticeships, and global technology hackathons.

Academic Departments

Our institute currently offers specialized academic training across six established departments:

  • Department of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence
  • Department of Software Engineering & Cyber Systems
  • Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering
  • Department of Mechanical & Mechatronics Engineering
  • Department of Civil & Environmental Infrastructure
  • Department of Biomedical Engineering & Health Informatics

Undergraduate & Graduate Admission Steps

All candidates seeking admission to degree programs must complete the following six steps:

  1. Create an applicant account on the official Horizon Crest Online Admissions Portal.
  2. Submit certified secondary school certificates, academic transcripts, and identity documentation.
  3. Appear for the mandatory Horizon Crest College Aptitude & Entrance Test (HCAAT).
  4. Participate in the departmental interview and faculty counseling session.
  5. Receive the merit-based provisional admission offer letter and accept enrollment.
  6. Submit enrollment verification fees and attend the New Student Orientation Week.
