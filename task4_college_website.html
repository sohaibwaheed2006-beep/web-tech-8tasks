About Horizon Crest Institute

Welcome to Horizon Crest Institute of Technology, an internationally acclaimed center for higher education and cutting-edge engineering research. Established in 1994, our institution is committed to nurturing intellectual curiosity and preparing the next generation of engineers, computer scientists, and technology innovators.

Our sprawling state-of-the-art campus houses modern digital research laboratories, high-performance computing clusters, collaborative makerspaces, and advanced sports amenities. We firmly believe that rigorous academic fundamentals combined with experiential learning empower graduates to thrive in rapidly transforming global industries.

With over 15,000 alumni worldwide and collaborations with leading multinational technology organizations, Horizon Crest continues to set benchmarks in academic rigor, patent contributions, and graduate employability.

Academic Departments

Horizon Crest Institute comprises six distinguished academic departments:

  • Department of Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence
  • Department of Software Engineering & Cyber Systems
  • Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering
  • Department of Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering
  • Department of Civil & Environmental Infrastructure
  • Department of Biomedical Engineering & Health Informatics

Admissions Procedure

Prospective candidates must complete the following six sequential admission steps:

  1. Register on the official Horizon Crest Online Admissions Portal and create an applicant profile.
  2. Submit certified academic records, secondary school certificates, and standardized test transcripts.
  3. Appear for the comprehensive Horizon Crest College Admission & Aptitude Test (HCAAT).
  4. Attend an academic advisory and interview session with department faculty representatives.
  5. Receive the merit-based provisional admission offer letter and accept the enrollment agreement.
  6. Complete tuition fee clearance, submit health records, and participate in Freshman Orientation Week.
